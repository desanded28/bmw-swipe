## 1. Which outfit is it?

### Answer: the "Britannian outfit" (ブリタニア衣装), sold as "C.C. Empress", "C.C. Empress Ver." or "C.C. Queen"
- **It is not in the anime.** It is an official illustration outfit by character designer **Takahiro Kimura**. It depicts C.C. after Lelouch becomes the **99th Emperor of Britannia**, and it is designed as the partner to Lelouch's white Emperor outfit. Toy-people says it "has appeared multiple times in official illustrations but never in the anime series itself". — [toy-people (EN) announcement](https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=72828); [toy-people (EN) painted sample](https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=72666); [toy-people (JP)](https://www.toy-people.com/jp/?p=72666)
- **In the anime itself**, C.C. is back in the white straitjacket at the end of S1 and in the R2 finale, during Lelouch's reign and Zero Requiem. Lelouch and Suzaku got new outfits, but she did not. — [pixiv 百科事典: C.C.](https://dic.pixiv.net/a/C.C.)
- So there is **no in-anime "white-and-gold C.C. empress dress" from R2 ep. 22–25**. The fan "empress/queen" look is this official-art black gown. The white-and-gold scheme is **Lelouch's** Emperor outfit.
- **Official 3D reference:** MegaHouse Precious G.E.M. "C.C. Britannia Outfit ver." (ブリタニア衣装ver.), 1/8 scale, about 260 mm, ¥27,500. It was released in Feb/Apr 2023 and also sold as a set with the Emperor Lelouch figure for ¥46,200. Its product photos are the best colour and detail reference. — [animeanime.jp](https://animeanime.jp/article/2022/07/15/70814.html); [MyFigureCollection 1576754](https://myfigurecollection.net/item/1576754); [Otakumode listing](https://otakumode.com/shop/62c78701a1560d0020bc6c2e/Precious-G-E-M-Series-Code-Geass-Lelouch-of-the-Rebellion-C-C-Britannian-Outfit-Ver); [eBay.de figure resale](https://www.ebay.de/itm/204512971971)

## 4. Delivery and customs math

- **Window:** ordering Mon 12 or Tue 13 Oct leaves about **13 German working days** to Thu 29 Oct (Oct 31 Reformationstag is not a holiday in Bavaria).
- **eBay SpeedPAK Standard (China to DE):** 8–12 working days *after the seller hands off the parcel*. Observed figures for Germany are around 15 days, and some trackers say 10–25 days, with customs adding 1–5 days. With 1–3 days seller handling, it lands **around Oct 26–Nov 3: borderline**. Only buy if the listing's own estimate to a Munich PLZ (80xxx) ends **on or before 29 Oct**. — [eBay SpeedPAK export page](https://export.ebay.com/sea-en/speedpak/speedpak-shipping-services); [parcelsapp SpeedPAK](https://parcelsapp.com/en/carriers/speedpak); [ship24](https://ship24.com/couriers/speedpak-tracking)
- **AliExpress Choice:** about 5–15 working days to DE. Only items with a delivery promise date shown at checkout of ≤ 29 Oct are realistic.
- **Made-to-order shops** (Hallowcos, Takerlama, Cosrea, Gvavaya, custom eBay sets): 10–30 days processing plus shipping. **They will miss 31 Oct.**
- **Customs:** since **1 Jul 2026** the EU charges a **€3 customs duty per item category** in low-value (< €150) non-EU parcels. It is temporary, until about Jul 2028. This comes **plus 19 % German import VAT**. eBay listings that say "Einfuhrabgaben inklusive" (IOSS) collect this at checkout. Otherwise expect the carrier to collect VAT, duty and a handling fee on delivery, which also slows delivery. — [European Commission, 29 Jun 2026](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/ensuring-fairness-and-safety-eur3-customs-duty-low-value-parcels-2026-06-29_en); [VATCalc](https://vatcalc.com/eu/eu-e3-levy-low-value-e-commerce-import-package-july-2026)
- A separate EU customs handling fee (around €2) has been discussed for later in 2026. Its status is unconfirmed.

## 5. Bottom line / shortlist

1. **The outfit she means:** C.C.'s official-art **Britannian / "Empress" outfit**. It is a black full-length gown with white strips hanging from the waist, gold or metallic eye-motif ornaments and crystal pendants, detached sleeves, a neck ornament, a crown, earrings and stockings. It is the partner to Emperor Lelouch's white-and-gold outfit, and it never appears in the anime.
2. **Budget reality:** **I found no true Empress set under €60 delivered.** The cheapest plausible option is the eBay "C.C. Ceremonial Dress" at **≈ €80 delivered**, with import fees included, but the design must be confirmed from its photos. Next is the eBay.de "Queen Clothing" set at **≈ €97**. Proper Empress sets with crown and sleeves start at about €92 (Uwowo, sold out) and run about €112–170, mostly made to order, so too late.
3. **Emperor Lelouch:** eBay.de timyuan2008 at **≈ €95 delivered** (no returns). AliExpress snippets show €48–63, which is unverified.
4. If the budget really caps at about €60 for her, or nothing ready-made can promise delivery by 29 Oct, there are two fallbacks:
   - a DIY approximation: a black maxi dress, white satin ribbon strips at the waist, gold trim, eye-motif appliqués or printouts, detached sleeves, a tiara and a €7–30 green wig;
   - switching to a cheaper C.C. look (see `../Lelouch and CC cosplay in Munich/online_ready_made.md` and `diy_thrift.md`).
