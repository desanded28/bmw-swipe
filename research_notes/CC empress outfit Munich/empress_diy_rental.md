## 0. Canon reference: C.C.'s Britannia ("Empress / Queen") outfit

### Takeaway
The outfit comes from official illustrations designed by Takahiro Kimura. It pairs with Emperor Lelouch's outfit and **never appears in the anime**. In the anime itself, C.C. went back to her straitjacket during the Emperor arc.

The design:
- a **black floor-length gown**
- **white strip-panels hanging from the waist**
- many **metallic (gold) eye-shaped ornaments** and **crystal pendants**

The coordinator's reference brief also lists a **crown or headpiece, detached sleeves, a neck piece, earrings and stockings**. I could not open the figure pages to confirm the colours of the sleeves and stockings, so check them against the MegaHouse photos.

### Cited findings
- MegaHouse Precious G.E.M. "C.C. Britannia Outfit ver." (1/8 scale; ¥27,500; announced for Feb 2023) is the first 3D version of an outfit "that appeared multiple times in official illustrations but never in the anime". The design is a black full-length gown with striped white skirt panels hanging from the waist and eye-shaped ornaments all over. It is designed to pair with Lelouch's Emperor attire. ## 1. C.C. Britannia gown: piece-by-piece (costs and Munich sources)

### Takeaway
The **cheapest realistic build is about €25–35** (excluding the wig):
- a **second-hand black long dress from Kleinanzeigen München** (snapshots at **€8–20**) or a new **H&M jersey maxi (€12.99)** / **Primark long-sleeve maxi (€16)**
- **one roll of white satin ribbon** (about €3–5 for 25 m) for the waist strips
- **Action's EVA foam floor mats (6 × 40×40 cm for €6.97)**, painted gold (€2–4), for the eye ornaments, neck piece and crown
- a **€1–5 crown** (Action Halloween headband €0.99; Horror-Shop gold glitter crown €4.95; Müller Fries diadem €4.99)
- **detached sleeves** from black tulle (about €2–4/m) or cut from a thrifted black top
- cheap stockings and earrings

**Shortcut base:** Horror-Shop's **"Braut der Finsternis"** is a floor-length black velvet gown with a white satin front panel, already close to the canon black-and-white palette, for **€27.95** (S/M) **[snapshot]**.

**No-sew steps (Lelouch)** [estimate / method]:
1. Fold the lapels into a stand collar and glue or pin them, or glue an EVA strip inside the collar.
2. Glue gold ribbon along the front edges, hem, cuffs and collar. Clip with clothes-pegs and let dry overnight.
3. Cut EVA epaulettes, paint them gold, and pin them on.
4. Pleat one edge of the sheet and safety-pin it under the epaulettes as the mantle. Keep the factory hems and glue gold ribbon to the front edges and the bottom.
5. Make an EVA cylinder hat, paint it white or cover it in white fabric, and add a gold band and an eye emblem (gold foam with a red gem).
6. Finish with white trousers and white gloves.

## 5. C.C. no-sew build steps (black Britannia gown) [estimate / method]
1. **Gown:** a black floor-length dress (or Horror-Shop "Braut der Finsternis"). Check that the hem reaches the floor in the shoes she will wear.
2. **Waist strips:** cut 6–10 white satin-ribbon strips (38–50 mm) or white sheet strips (8–10 cm) to floor length. Glue the tops to a black ribbon or elastic waistband and tie it on, or safety-pin the strips inside the waistline so the dress itself is not glued (good if the dress is to be resold).
3. **Eye ornaments:**
   - Trace almond "eye" shapes (about 6–10 cm) on the EVA mat and cut them with a sharp craft knife.
   - Seal them with PVA or white glue, then paint two coats of gold acrylic.
   - Glue a red or clear gem as the iris and hang an acrylic crystal drop below on a jump ring or thread.
   - Pin them to the bodice, waist and skirt with safety pins glued to the back. Hot glue directly on thin jersey can melt or mark it [general technique; not sourced].
4. **Neck piece:** a gold-painted EVA collar or choker with a central eye, or a gold statement necklace.
5. **Crown:** the Horror-Shop gold glitter crown (€4.95) or Müller Dark Angel diadem (€4.99), or an EVA crown painted gold with an eye in the middle. A €0.99 Action headband works as the base.
6. **Detached sleeves:** a tulle or chiffon tube with an elastic top, or cut-off top or tight legs.
7. **Earrings + stockings + green wig.** Shoes: own black heels or flats.
- **Drying:** textile glue needs about 24 h to cure fully, and acrylic on EVA about 1–2 h per coat. Do the painting on day 1 and the assembly on day 2.
