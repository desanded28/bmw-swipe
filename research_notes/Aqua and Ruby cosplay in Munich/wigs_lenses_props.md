## 0. Character reference: which eye has the star, eye colours, hair

### Takeaway
- **Ruby:** the star is in her **LEFT eye**, and her eyes are **pink / purplish-pink**. In later arcs a second star appears in her right eye. Her stars are white when things are normal and turn **black/dark** during her revenge arc.
- **Aqua:** the star is in his **RIGHT eye**. It is often drawn **dark/black** because of his revenge storyline; it started bright. His eye colour was **not confirmed** by any character source. Lens sellers market "Aqua Hoshino" lenses as **blue / aqua-blue with a white star**.
- **Recommendation for a recognisable Halloween look:** one white star per person, Aqua's on the right and Ruby's on the left, so the pair mirror each other.
- **Ruby's hair:** long, straight, blonde, with bangs. Fan sources say she often wears a **small side ponytail/tuft**, which echoes her mother Ai's. **No reliable source shows a ribbon.**
- **Aqua's hair:** short blonde. Several wig listings describe a subtle **pink or orange tint** mixed in.

### Inferences
- Ruby's side tuft can be faked on any long blonde wig: gather a small section above one ear with a clear elastic. Alternatively buy the clip-on ponytail version.
- A ribbon is not needed for recognisability.
- **The star is the single most recognisable element of the pair**, so it is worth getting right on the correct side.

### Gaps
- No official character page could be opened; the Fandom wiki is blocked.
- Aqua's right-eye star rests on one secondary source.
- Aqua's base iris colour comes from lens marketing, not from a character source.

**Regulation**
- **EU MDR Annex XVI** covers contact lenses without a medical purpose. Since **22 June 2023** they fall under the MDR, subject to transitional rules. — [Hippokratech](https://hippokratech.substack.com/p/hippokratech-news-und-trends-f96); [Johner Institut](https://www.johner-institut.de/blog/regulatory-affairs/produkte-ohne-medizinische-zweckbestimmung/) (from 12 Oct notes)
- An optician-association info sheet says retailers must check that lens and care-product packaging carry a **CE mark**, and that a manufacturer's declaration of conformity exists. — [WVAO Infoblatt MDR Kontaktlinsen (PDF)](https://www.wvao.org/wp-content/uploads/2021_05_Infoblatt-MDR_Kontaktlinsen.pdf)
- No prescription is legally required in Germany for plano coloured lenses, but a fitting is recommended (retailer guide). — [lensbest.de](https://www.lensbest.de/blog/farbige-kontaktlinsen-ohne-sehstaerke-ein-ratgeber) (from 12 Oct notes)
- A manufacturer's own CE claims should be checked against the notified-body certificate or EUDAMED before you trust them.
- I found no source on German customs rules for private lens imports.

**Safety (German professional bodies)**
- **ZVA (opticians):**
  - Halloween lenses should be professionally fitted "selbst wenn die Kontaktlinsen nur stundenweise getragen werden". The ZVA warns against lenses "aus dem Internet oder der Drogerie".
  - Motif lenses with an intensive colour layer let too little oxygen reach the eye, so do not wear them long.
  - **Not for driving:** the clear opening is about 5 mm, while the pupil widens to about 8 mm in the dark.
  - Never swap lenses, keep them away from water, and wash your hands before handling.
  - — [Deutsche Handwerks Zeitung](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=147841); [Handwerksblatt](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/panorama/vorsicht-bei-farbigen-kontaktlinsen-zu-halloween)
- **DOG (ophthalmologists):**
  - Wearing fun/crazy lenses too long or with poor hygiene risks corneal injury and infection.
  - Wear them **only a few hours**, and remove them immediately if the eye turns red, burns or waters.
  - — [Ärzte Zeitung](https://www.aerztezeitung.de/Medizin/Problematischer-Hingucker-308740.html) (from 12 Oct notes; no new 2025/2026 DOG statement surfaced this session)
- **aponet (pharmacy portal, 29 Oct 2025):** coloured lenses are often underestimated as an accessory rather than a medical product. — [aponet.de](https://www.aponet.de/artikel/grusel-linsen-zu-halloween-darauf-sollten-sie-bei-kontaktlinsen-achten-12094)
