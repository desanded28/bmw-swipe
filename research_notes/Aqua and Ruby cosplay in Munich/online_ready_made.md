# Ready-made Aqua and Ruby (Oshi no Ko) costumes online, delivered to Munich by Halloween 2026

> **Method note (read first):** Research ran on Thu 15 Oct 2026. Halloween is Sat 31 Oct 2026, so 16 days remain including today. In practice about 12 working days remain for delivery. As instructed, no direct page fetches were attempted. **Every price and detail below comes from search-engine snapshots and summaries of the indexed pages, not from a live product page.** Treat every price as **"search-index snapshot, verify"**. Snapshot dates are mostly unknown, and some pages are a year or more old (e.g. an EZCosplay page was flagged ~504 days old). USD→EUR uses **0.86 €/$**, so converted figures are **estimates**. **No Amazon.de delivery date, Prime badge or "Versand durch Amazon" line was visible in any snapshot.** Each Amazon "safe" rating below therefore depends on you seeing a delivery date of 28 Oct or earlier on the live page. About 40 web searches were run.

**Risk scale used:**
- **SAFE**: Amazon.de with a delivery date of 28 Oct or earlier.
- **MODERATE**: AliExpress Choice listing showing 27 Oct or earlier.
- **HIGH**: other shipping from China, including eBay China sellers, in-stock items from China-based "German" shops, and AliExpress non-Choice.
- **WILL MISS**: made-to-order items.

**Customs budget:** since 1 July 2026 the EU charges €3 duty per product category (6-digit tariff code) on non-EU parcels under €150, plus 19% VAT. Budget **≈ €3.60 per item** where it isn't already included in the price. Sources: [MaltaToday](https://www.maltatoday.com.mt/news/world/138717/eu_introduces_3_tax_on_parcels_from_shein_temu); [Cyprus Mail](https://cyprus-mail.com/2025/12/12/eu-to-impose-e3-duty-on-e-commerce-parcels-from-july-2026); [mydealz thread "Neue Zoll-Modalitäten ab 1.7.2026 … mind. 3 EUR Pauschalgebühr pro Warengruppe"](https://www.mydealz.de/diskussion/neue-zoll-modalitaten-ab-172026-ohne-schohnfrist-mind-3eur-pauschalgebuhr-pro-warengruppe-ab-001eur-wert-2797424). An anecdotal mydealz post says AliExpress prices rose from 1 Aug 2026, which suggests the duty is often built into the checkout price ([mydealz](https://www.mydealz.de/diskussion/aliexpress-ab-01082026-berechnet-gefuhlt-3-fache-preis-2816709)). Items fulfilled by Amazon are already in the EU, so nothing extra is due on them.

## Q0. What the target outfits look like (for matching listings)

- **Youtou High uniform.** An image-model tag list describes it as a blue cropped open jacket, black vest, white shirt, pink bow tie and grey skirt for the girls' version ([comfy.icu](https://comfy.icu/models/1201009/-IllustriousXL-v0-1-Ruby-Hoshino-Oshi-no-Ko)). The Nendoroid of Ruby's "Yoto High School" uniform shows "the prominent bow on her chest" ([toy-people](https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=91263)). A Youtou-themed Innocent World dress uses a large pink ribbon ([lolibrary](https://lolibrary.org/items/iw-youtou-high-school-ribbon-op-oshi-no-ko-collaboration)). **So for Ruby, look for a blue jacket, white shirt, pink bow and grey skirt. For Aqua, look for a blue jacket, white shirt, tie and trousers.** Listings titled "Blaue Uniform" match. "Matrosen/Seemannsanzug" (sailor-suit) listings are a different, less accurate look.
- **Aqua's Tokyo Blade role is "Touki".** Kana plays Tsurugi ([Anime News Network](https://awww.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2024-03-23/oshi-no-ko-anime-2nd-season-reveals-july-debut-more-cast-in-video/.209132)).
- **Ruby later plays Ai** in the film arc (manga ch. 116) ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/anime/oshi-no-ko-chapter-116-ruby-set-play-ai-hoshino-akane-plans-stopping-aqua)). That makes an Ai-style idol dress with Ruby's **blonde** wig a defensible Ruby costume.
- **Aqua casual hoodie/jacket:** no source describing it, and no dedicated listing, was found (see Q1).
