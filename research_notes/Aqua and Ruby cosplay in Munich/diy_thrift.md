## 4. No-sew methods (for builders with minimal sewing skills)

### Takeaway
Two adhesives cover everything:
- **Textile glue** (Gütermann HT2 or Aleene's style) for anything worn against the body or likely to flex: trim, lapels, ruffles.
- **Hot glue** for stiff decorations: stars, bows, boot-cover bows, the light stick.

The tutu is knotted, with no glue or sewing at all. Hot glue isn't recommended on garments that will be washed, and too much fabric glue soaks through and stiffens thin fabric, so always test on a hidden seam first.

### A. Uniform trim with ribbon and fabric glue (Aqua and Ruby jackets)
1. Lay the blazer flat and iron the trim ribbon.
2. **Lapels (Aqua):** trace each lapel onto paper, cut out a template, and cut black felt or satin about 3 mm smaller than the lapel. Glue it down with a thin, even layer of HT2 and smooth it flat. Weight it under books.
3. **Gold trim:** run a **thin line** of glue along the back of the 10 mm gold ribbon, about 30 cm at a time. Press it onto the lapel edge, front opening and cuffs, about 1–3 cm from the cuff edge for Ruby's "stripes at the end of the sleeves". Hold it with clothespins or pins while it dries. Aleene's advice is that **less glue is better**, because too much soaks through. — [Aleene's no-sew with fabric glue](https://www.aleenes.com/project/no-sew-fringe-clothing-diy-with-fabric-glue); [eHow: how to glue trim on fabric](https://www.ehow.com/how_5878998_glue-trim-fabric.html)
4. At corners, fold the ribbon into a mitre, add a dot of glue, and clamp.
5. Fold the cut ends under 5 mm and glue them so they don't fray. A dab of clear nail polish also works.
6. Let it cure overnight and don't wash it. Fabric glue and fusible tape suit washable garments; hot glue suits decorative pieces that won't be laundered. — [eHow](https://www.ehow.com/how_5878998_glue-trim-fabric.html)
7. **Cropping Ruby's jacket, optional and reversible:** fold the hem up inside to the length you want and fuse it with iron-on hem tape (Saumvlies/Saumfix, €2.99–7.49) [snapshot, verify]. Test glue or tape on a hidden area first, because it can stain or show through thin fabric. — [Maria Sew no-sew hem methods](https://mariasew.com/?p=5487)

### B. Idol skirt: knotted tulle on an elastic waistband (no-sew tutu)
1. **Elastic:** cut 2–3 cm wide non-roll elastic to the **waist measurement minus about 5 cm (2 in)**. Overlap the ends and close them with a safety pin or a knot, or with a dab of hot glue plus a safety pin for strength. Slip it over a chair back, a cushion or your knee to keep it stretched while you work. — [Stelle World](https://stelleworld.com/blogs/kids-dance-tips/diy-no-sew-tutu); [Peanut Blossom](https://www.peanutblossom.com/?p=7627)
2. **Strips:** cut tulle into strips 15 cm wide (15 cm rolls are already the right width) and **twice the finished length + 5 cm** long. A 35 cm skirt needs strips of about 75 cm. — [Stelle World](https://stelleworld.com/blogs/kids-dance-tips/diy-no-sew-tutu)
3. **Knot:** fold a strip in half and put the loop under the elastic. Bring the tails over the elastic and through the loop, then pull snug. A slip knot or "lark's head" works. Push the knots tight together and alternate pink and white. — [Instructables No Sew Tutu](https://www.instructables.com/No-Sew-Tutu); [Peanut Blossom](https://www.peanutblossom.com/?p=7627)
4. **Quantity:** a full standalone adult tutu can take about **80 yards (73 m)** of 15 cm strips, roughly 9–10 rolls of 9 m. Another guide gives 6–15 yards for a less full one. — [Stelle World](https://stelleworld.com/blogs/kids-dance-tips/diy-no-sew-tutu)
   - **Overlay [inference]:** worn as an overlay over the base dress's skirt, **4–6 rolls (36–54 m) is enough**, which is about 50–70 strips. For 150 cm tulle off the bolt, a 75 cm cut makes 10 strips, so about 4–5 m covers an overlay.
5. Trim the hem straight or in points, and wear the tutu over the dress at the natural waist. Cover the elastic with a satin ribbon sash tied in a big bow at the back.
6. **Ruffles:** fold 10 cm wide tulle strips in half lengthwise and run a thin line of textile glue along the fold. Pleat them every 2–3 cm with your fingers and press onto the dress neckline, sleeve hems or skirt hem. Pre-gathered lace trim glues on the same way.

### C. Stars from glitter foam or HTV
- **Glitter foam (best choice for this build):** draw a star template on card, about 6–8 cm for the skirt and 3–4 cm for the hair and shoes. Trace it on the back of the foam and cut it out with sharp scissors or a craft knife.
  - Attach to the dress with textile glue or hot glue.
  - Attach to tulle with a small dot of hot glue, sandwiching the tulle between two stars. Glue soaks straight through tulle, so put baking paper underneath.
  - Self-adhesive foam sticks well to hair clips, the light stick and boot covers.
- **Glitter HTV (Flexfolie), cut by hand:** no good hand-cut tutorial turned up, because every result assumed a Cricut or Silhouette. Method [inference, based on general HTV practice]:
  1. Draw the star **mirrored** on the shiny carrier side.
  2. Cut through both layers with scissors.
  3. Place it glitter-side up, carrier on top, onto **cotton or polyester only** (the base dress, the vest, the hoodie).
  4. Press with a dry household iron on the cotton/wool setting, no steam, for about 15–20 s under baking paper. Peel as the sheet's instructions say (warm or cold).
  - **Don't iron HTV onto tulle** (it melts and scorches). Check the manufacturer's temperature on the sheet. — HTV search summary: [guide noting iron vs press](https://openpension.ctdata.org/how-to-cut-iron-on-vinyl) (low-quality source; use the product sheet)
- **Ready-made iron-on stars** (€1.29–5.99 each) [snapshot, verify] are the zero-skill fallback for the base dress.

### D. Bows (hair bows, uniform ribbon, boot bows)
- **Two-layer ribbon bow:**
  1. Glue a thin ribbon on top of a wide one.
  2. Fold the ends to the middle to make two loops, and pinch the centre.
  3. Wrap a short strip around the middle and hot-glue it at the back.
  4. Glue a hair clip or bobby pin behind it. — [Instructables No-Sew Hair Bow](https://instructables.com/2-Layer-Hair-Bow)
- **Fabric bow, accordion style** (for Ruby's large uniform ribbon): fold a rectangle of satin into a tube and glue the long edge. Pleat it in the middle like an accordion and wrap a strip around the centre. Glue a safety pin or brooch pin behind it and pin it at the collar. — [WhatsUpMoms DIY No-Sew Hair Bow](https://whatsupmoms.com/for-mom/diy/no-sew-hair-bow/); [Sweet Red Poppy no-sew fabric bows](https://sweetredpoppy.com/?p=3141); [The Paper Mama no-sew bow](https://thepapermama.com/2011/11/50-diy-days-no-sew-bow/)
- **Ruby's draped "ribbon tie":** make a two-loop bow from 50 mm ribbon or a satin strip and let two long tails (20–25 cm) hang down. Cut the tail ends at an angle and seal them with a lighter flame or nail polish.

### E. Boot covers and light stick (no-sew)
- **Boot covers:** pull white or pink knee socks or leg warmers over plain ankle boots or white trainers and glue a ribbon bow and a foam star to the front. To make it neater, cut the toe off long white socks so they sit as cuffs over the shoes [inference].
- **Light stick:** buy a red glow stick or LED wand and hot-glue a glitter-foam star to the tip. Wrap the handle in red or pink satin ribbon.

## 7. Gaps and caveats
- **No in-store prices** were indexed for Action, TEDi, Müller, idee., Primark, KiK, H&M or C&A Munich; the benchmarks are marketplace listings. Thrift garment prices are estimates. The previous Munich research only noted that T-shirts at Weißer Rabe are often around €2.
- **The idol dress's canonical colours are unconfirmed.** The debut costume is "gothic-style" (KDcolle). Merchants show both a white/black version with red gloves and a pink "Season 2" stage dress. Check an episode screenshot before buying the base dress.
- **The pink ribbon on Ruby's uniform** rests on secondary sources (fan wiki, AI tag list, Innocent World collab).
- **Aqua's casual look** isn't documented in canon sources found here; it's a fan interpretation (dark hoodie or coat, dark trousers).
- **Kleinanzeigen and Vinted** listings are barely indexed, so do a live search in the apps.
- **No good hand-cut HTV tutorial** was found; the HTV method above is general practice, so check the product sheet.
- **Stock at Horror-Shop City Store** (boot covers, light sticks) is unverified.
