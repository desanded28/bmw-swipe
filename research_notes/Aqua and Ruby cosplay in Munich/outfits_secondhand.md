## Part 1: Outfit anatomy

### Takeaway
- Aqua's one practical outfit is the **Youtou High School uniform**: a blue blazer, white shirt, dark knit layer, tie and grey trousers, plus a short blond wig. It is warm, cheap to thrift and reads as a set next to Ruby.
- Ruby's **New B-Komachi debut stage costume** is the most eye-catching option, and Ruby's member colour is **red**, the same as Ai's. It is cold, though, and second-hand sets need luck on sizing.
- Ruby's **Youtou uniform** is the safe, warm-ish twin match.
- Two identifiers matter more than any garment: the **single star pupil** (Aqua's right eye, Ruby's left) and the **blond wigs** (short and messy for Aqua; long with a small one-side-up tail for Ruby).

**Photo tip [estimate]:** Stand with Aqua on the left of the frame and Ruby on the right. Aqua's right eye and Ruby's left eye then both sit on the outer edges of the pair, which gives a symmetric "twin stars" look.

**Star-pupil options [estimate]:**
1. A CE-marked cosmetic star lens in **one eye only** (Aqua right, Ruby left). It must come from a reputable shop and be worn within its time limits. Skip it if either of you has never worn contacts.
2. The cheapest non-canon cue: a small white star face-sticker or eyeliner star beside the correct eye, for Aqua's "dark star" a black one. A black star suits Halloween.
3. No lens at all. The wigs plus the pairing still carry the look in photos, especially next to a B-Komachi penlight.

### 1.2 Aqua's outfits

**A. Youtou High School uniform (performing-arts and general high school, Tokyo)**
- The school is real in-story: Youtou is one of the few high schools offering a performing-arts track [cited: [Oshi no Ko Wiki: Youtou High School](https://oshinoko.fandom.com/wiki/Youtou_High_School)].
- **Blazer:** blue (mid/cobalt-to-cornflower blue). A blue jacket for the Youtou uniform appears consistently in fan tags [fan-tag: [civarchive Aqua LoRA](https://civarchive.com/seaart/models/c515bcf33f47c6980f549856676be163/versions/288bd0e6e1a6b87af8cb718314328fac)]. **Trim / piping not confirmed** [verify against figure photos].
- **Under-layer:** white collared shirt plus a **black/dark knit sweater or vest** [fan-tag: "black sweater"].
- **Tie:** the fan tags say a **red** tie (tagged "bowtie") [fan-tag]. Whether it is a necktie or a bow, and its exact red, is unconfirmed [memory/verify]. A dark-red necktie is the safe budget choice and echoes Ruby's member colour.
- **Trousers:** grey (charcoal/mid-grey) [memory/verify; the fan tags list a grey skirt for the girls' version].
- **Shoes:** brown or black loafers [fan-tag: "brown loafers", memory/verify].
- **References to check colours:** Nendoroid Aqua in uniform (comes with smartphone, camera, penlight and B-Komachi support board) [cited: [animecorner](https://animecorner.me/good-smile-company-set-to-release-aqua-hoshino-and-kana-arima-nendoroid-from-oshi-no-ko/)]; POP UP PARADE Aqua in school attire [cited: [BBTS](https://bbts.com/Product/VariationDetails/269040)]; Chokonose Premium Aqua uniform [[jumpichiban](https://jumpichiban.com/products/oshi-no-ko-choco-nose-premium-figure-aqua-uniform)]. The official Innocent World x Oshi no Ko "Youtou High School blade jacket" (2024) is an official uniform-inspired reference [cited: [Lolibrary](https://lolibrary.org/items/iw-youtou-high-school-blade-jacket-oshi-no-ko-collaboration)].

**B. Casual: dark hoodie or jacket**
- Shown as a dark (black/navy) hoodie or jacket over a plain tee with dark trousers, sometimes with a cap [memory/verify]. No figure or source describing it was found.
- It is only recognisable through the wig and star eye, or when standing next to idol Ruby, ideally holding **red (Ruby), white (Kana) and yellow (MEM-cho) penlights** as in ep. 11 [cited: [Oshi no Ko Wiki ep. 11](https://oshinoko.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_11)].

**C. Tokyo Blade stage play: Touki**
- Aqua plays **Touki** in the Lala Lai Theatrical Company's *Tokyo Blade* stage adaptation (Season 2). Kana plays Tsurugi [cited: [myanime mirror synopsis](https://myanime.it.com/anime/oshi-no-ko-2nd-season)].
- Figures show him "clad in warrior robes" in a combat stance [cited: [Entertainment Earth Touki statue](https://www.entertainmentearth.com/product/oshi-no-ko-aqua-hoshino-touki-version-statue/san89691)]. There is also a Banpresto "Sword Demon ver." (12 cm, Jan 2025) [cited: [jumpichiban](https://jumpichiban.com/products/oshi-no-ko-aqua-sword-demon-ver)].
- Exact colours were **not found**. My recollection is a dark Japanese warrior outfit: kimono-style top, hakama-style trousers, sash and a katana prop [memory/verify]. Use the figures above as the colour reference.
- **Practical note [estimate]:** Carry a light foam or wooden prop sword in a bag on the U-Bahn. Avoid realistic metal replicas in public.

**D. Other roles and looks (low value for Halloween)**
- Child Aqua and the early child-actor role, the dating-reality-show arc ("Love Now") and the *Sweet Today* cameo are all ordinary clothes [memory/verify]. They are not recognisable as costumes, so skip them.

### 1.3 Ruby's outfits

**A. Youtou High School girls' uniform**
- **Blue cropped, open jacket** with long sleeves, a **black vest**, a **white collared shirt**, a **pink/red bow tie** and a **grey (pleated) skirt** [fan-tag: [comfy.icu Ruby LoRA](https://comfy.icu/models/1201009/-IllustriousXL-v0-1-Ruby-Hoshino-Oshi-no-Ko)].
- Legs and shoes: dark socks plus loafers [memory/verify]. For October, wear **thick black tights**.
- References: the Nendoroid School Uniform Ver. (Dec 2024) [cited: [toy-people](https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=94571)], the Coreful figure in school uniform [[BBTS](https://www.bigbadtoystore.com/product/oshi-ko-ruby-hoshino-school-uniform-ver-coreful-figure-136594)], and the official Innocent World "Youtou High School ribbon OP" [[Lolibrary](https://lolibrary.org/items/iw-youtou-high-school-ribbon-op-oshi-no-ko-collaboration)].
- Cheap ready-made sets exist as "OSHI NO KO Hoshino Rubii / Arima Kana JK Uniform Komplettset" (new, from a China seller, about US$51 plus US$9.99 shipping, sizes S–2XL) [cited: [eBay 364261501802](https://www.ebay.de/itm/364261501802)].

**B. New B-Komachi debut stage costume (Japan Idol Festival, S1 ep. 11)**
- **Style:** a "Gothic-style performance costume" with a **multi-layered design, ruffles and frills** [cited: [toy-people KDcolle Ruby 1/7](https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=83704)].
- **Colours:** **not confirmed by any snippet.** The matching KDcolle Kana figure is described as a "**burgundy** Gothic-style performance outfit", which suggests a dark wine/burgundy group costume with lighter frills [inference/verify]. Ruby's member colour is **red** [cited: [ch. 38](https://oshinoko.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_38)].
- **Star motifs:** not confirmed for this costume [verify on figure photos].
- **Hair accessory and headwear:** not confirmed. The used Berlin "Ruby Hoshino Cosplay Komplettset" lists Perücke, Haarnetz, Jacke, Oberteil, Rock, Schleife, **Mütze** and Socken (wig, hairnet, jacket, top, skirt, bow, **hat** and socks). That suggests the idol set includes a small hat or headpiece [inference from listing contents].
- **Props:** a **microphone**, a **glow stick/penlight** and a fan; the Nendoroid ships with exactly these [cited: [otakumart Nendoroid #2271](https://otakumart.co.nz/products/oshi-no-ko-ruby-nendoroid-2271), [Siliconera](https://siliconera.com/?p=975243)]. Cheap substitutes [estimate]: a red glow stick for €1–3 and a toy or karaoke mic for €5–10.

**C. Ai-style idol outfit**
- Ai's iconic B-Komachi stage dress is pink, magenta and white, with layered frills and bows, and her signature **rabbit-ear + star hair clip** [clip cited via eBay listings above; dress colours memory/verify; reference: [Prime 1 Studio Ai](https://prime1studio.com/onk-ai/PWONK-01PS.html) mentions "satin and sheer fabrics" and stage colours].
- Ruby performs in Ai's image later in the story; she plays Ai in the in-story film [memory/verify]. Wearing Ai's dress with a **blond** wig and a **left-eye** star therefore reads as "Ruby as Ai".
- **Why it matters for budget:** second-hand stock of **Ai** costumes is much larger than stock of Ruby's (see Part 2), and Ai is the most recognisable figure to non-fans because of YOASOBI's "Idol" [estimate].

**D. Casual looks**
- Examples are the "Date Style" spring, cherry-blossom outfit [cited: [jumpichiban Date Style](https://jumpichiban.com/de/products/oshi-no-ko-figure-ruby-hoshino-date-style-ver)] and the "POP IN 2" music-video outfit [cited: [jumpichiban POP IN 2](https://jumpichiban.com/products/oshi-no-ko-figure-ruby-hoshino-pop-in-2-ver)].
- Recognisability is low without the uniform or idol context.
- Other cosplay variants seen second-hand include an "Idol Military" Ruby and a cat-maid Ruby (see Part 2).

### 1.5 Recommended couple combos [estimate]

1. **"Twins at Youtou High": both in school uniform.** This is the cheapest (about €50–100 for both), the warmest, and the easiest to assemble in Munich thrift shops in 16 days. The blue jackets make it read as a matching set. Add one-eye stars and a red bow/tie for both. **Top pick for budget and comfort.**
2. **Idol Ruby (New B-Komachi debut) + casual Aqua with penlights.** Recreates ep. 11, where Aqua cheers in the crowd. Aqua stays warm and nearly free (wig only); Ruby gets the eye-catching costume. About €50–80 total if Ruby's set is found used. **Top pick for photos.**
3. **Idol Ruby + uniform Aqua.** A middle ground: Aqua looks clearly "in costume", Ruby is the star, about €60–100. It also works with an Ai-style dress if no debut set turns up in Ruby's size.
4. **"Ai's legacy": Ruby in Ai's idol dress + Aqua with a black (dark) star.** The most recognisable to non-fans and a fitting Halloween "revenge" vibe. Easiest to source second-hand on the Ruby side because Ai costumes are common.
- *Not recommended:* **Idol Ruby + Tokyo Blade Aqua.** It looks great but is the most expensive and hardest to source by 31 Oct. No used Touki costume was found anywhere.

**Cold-weather plan (both) [estimate]:** Heattech or thermal base layers, 100-den black tights for Ruby, a long dark coat to wear in transit and take off for photos, and a hand warmer in a pocket.

## Part 2: Second-hand route

### Takeaway
- **Ruby** cosplays are on Kleinanzeigen nationwide at about **€20–50**, mostly Uwowo-brand sets. Wigs alone run about €50.
- **Aqua** cosplays are almost absent second-hand. One Vinted DokiDoki Aqua set with wig in size S showed at **€33.50**, and nothing turned up on Kleinanzeigen or used eBay.
- **No Oshi no Ko cosplay was listed in Munich itself.** The nearest Bavarian hits are Nürnberg (Ai idol 3XL, Ruby maid 3XL) and an old Augsburg Ruby wig.
- Realistic plan: buy Ruby's set from a shipping seller with *Sicher bezahlen*, and **thrift-build Aqua's uniform in Munich** with a new short blond wig.

### 2.5 Buyer-protection fees (worked examples) [estimate, using the brief's fee model]
- **Kleinanzeigen "Sicher bezahlen":** €0.50 + 4.5% paid by the buyer.
  - €30 set: €0.50 + €1.35 = **€1.85**, so €31.85 plus shipping (about €5–7 [estimate]).
  - €50 set: €0.50 + €2.25 = **€2.75**, so €52.75 plus shipping.
- **Vinted Käuferschutz:** about 5% + €0.70, plus shipping.
  - €33.50 Aqua set: €0.70 + €1.68 = **€2.38**, so €35.88 plus shipping (about €4–7 [estimate]).
- Use the protected payment, not PayPal Friends or a bank transfer, for a shipped item from a stranger.
