## Item 3: Geass contact lenses (red/magenta), legality and safety

### Takeaway
- **Cheapest options:** plain red "crazy/fun" lenses without prescription strength are sold by German/EU shops from about €3–13 per pair (Funnylens) or about €7 per pair (OTTO marketplace brands).
- **True Geass-sigil lens:** the only one seen is a niche non-EU product (Mojosee, US$34.98 per pair). Delivery to Munich by 29 Oct is not assured.
- **Rules:** in the EU, coloured lenses without corrective power fall under MDR Annex XVI and have to meet medical-device-like requirements.
- **Medical advice:** German ophthalmologists (DOG, BVA) and opticians (ZVA) advise a professional fitting, only a few hours of wear, strict hygiene and never sharing lenses.

**Regulation (EU MDR Annex XVI):**
- Annex XVI explicitly covers "contact lenses or other items intended to be introduced into or onto the eye" without a medical purpose. These products face requirements similar to medical devices and must demonstrate safety and performance (Art. 61(9) MDR). — [Johner Institut](https://www.johner-institut.de/blog/regulatory-affairs/produkte-ohne-medizinische-zweckbestimmung/); [medizin-und-technik](https://medizin-und-technik.industrie.de/recht/annex-xvi-eu-mdr-ist-nicht-nur-fuer-medizinprodukte-relevant/)
- The German BVL confirms the new rules for Annex XVI products. It gives no information on prescription requirements. — [BVL Fachmeldung](https://www.bvl.bund.de/SharedDocs/Fachmeldungen/03_verbraucherprodukte/2021/2021_05_25_Neue_Regelungen_fuer_bestimmte_Prod_ohne_medizinische_Zweckbestimmung.html)
- Implementing regulations (EU) 2022/2346 and 2022/2347 apply the rules. One trade article gives 22 June 2023 as the applicability date, subject to transitional rules. — [Hippokratech](https://hippokratech.substack.com/p/hippokratech-news-und-trends-f96)
- No prescription is strictly required in Germany, but a fitting by an optician or ophthalmologist is recommended. This comes from a retailer guide. — [lensbest.de](https://www.lensbest.de/blog/farbige-kontaktlinsen-ohne-sehstaerke-ein-ratgeber)
- In the US, the FDA calls decorative lenses sold without a prescription illegal and warns they can cause serious injury and blindness. This is relevant if the user orders from US sellers. — [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/fda-skip-the-costume-contacts)

**Safety (German professional bodies):**
- **ZVA (opticians' association):** advises against long wear. Even lenses worn for a few hours should be professionally fitted, because costume-shop and drugstore lenses are not matched to the individual eye. — [Handwerksblatt](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/panorama/vorsicht-bei-farbigen-kontaktlinsen-zu-halloween); [Deutsche Handwerks Zeitung](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=147841)
- **DOG (German Ophthalmological Society):** wearing "fun/crazy" lenses too long or with poor hygiene risks serious corneal injury and infection. Wear them only a few hours and remove them immediately if the eyes become red, burn or water. Coloured lenses let particularly little oxygen through. — [Ärzte Zeitung](https://www.aerztezeitung.de/Medizin/Problematischer-Hingucker-308740.html)
- **Aponet (pharmacy portal, Oct 2025):** heavily pigmented lenses are less oxygen-permeable. Wash hands before handling, never swap lenses with others, and an optician can determine the exact diameter and curvature. — [aponet.de](https://www.aponet.de/artikel/grusel-linsen-zu-halloween-darauf-sollten-sie-bei-kontaktlinsen-achten-12094)
- **BVA (ophthalmologists' professional association):** warns that colour pigment often seals the lens pores, reducing oxygen. Keratitis (corneal inflammation) is a cited risk. — [de.coleyes.com guide quoting BVA](https://de.coleyes.com/blogs/seo/kontaktlinsen-gefaehrlich-ratgeber); [lensbest.de](https://www.lensbest.de/blog/farbige-kontaktlinsen-ohne-sehstaerke-ein-ratgeber)

### Inferences

- **Daily vs. long-wear:**
  - For a one-night event, a lens marked as a 1-day/daily lens is the safer choice: no cleaning regimen and no storage-contamination risk.
  - 3-month and yearly "crazy" lenses (like the LuxDelux yearly lens) need a solution and case, which adds cost (an estimate; no price seen).
- **Sigil lenses:** the Geass-sigil Mojosee lens is the only canon-accurate option seen. It is non-EU, its EU MDR conformity is unverified, and arrival by 29 Oct is uncertain, so it is not recommended under this budget and deadline.
- **Optician route:** visiting a Munich optician for a quick fitting check before wearing the lenses is the route recommended by the ZVA, DOG and aponet.
- **No-lens alternative (zero eye risk):** red/magenta eyeshadow or eyeliner around one eye, and a hand-drawn or printed Geass sigil on an eyepatch or sticker. Alternatively, add the red sigil eye in photo editing afterwards. No sourced product price; see Item 6 for the eyeshadow.

## Item 4: Zero mask/helmet (ready-made vs DIY), and whether it is worth it on a budget

- Ready-made wearable Zero helmets are expensive: about €100+ on eBay.de from Chinese sellers, US$60–160 elsewhere, and US$220–276 for custom Etsy builds. They usually ship from China, which is a real risk for the 29 Oct deadline.
- 3D-print files exist cheaply (about US$8–12, possibly free on Printables), but printing and finishing a full helmet takes time and access to a printer.
- No free Pepakura template was confirmed.
- On a tight budget and timeline, skipping the helmet, or making a quick cardboard/EVA-foam or paper version, is the pragmatic choice.

## Wig care tips (detangling, wig cap) and avoiding shiny, cheap-looking wigs

### Takeaway
- Wash a new synthetic wig before wearing it, because new wigs are shiniest.
- To cut shine, mist or comb through a little dry shampoo: a dark-hair formula for Lelouch's black wig, any formula for C.C.'s light green.
- Use matte powder or eyeshadow at the parting.
- Styling with some movement hides shine better than a flat, solid-colour wig.

### Cited Findings
- Dry shampoo is the most common quick de-shine fix. Use a dark-hair formula on black or dark wigs to avoid pale residue; any formula works on light wigs. — [wigs.com](https://www.wigs.com/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-wig-less-shiny); [gorod.it.com](https://gorod.it.com/?p=15125)
- Synthetic wigs are shiniest when new, so washing before wear reduces shine. — [Luxshine Hair](https://luxshinehair.com/?p=25730); [wigs.com](https://www.wigs.com/blogs/news/how-to-make-a-wig-less-shiny)
- Baby powder or talc reduces shine on grey, blonde and light-brown wigs only. — [Luxshine Hair](https://luxshinehair.com/?p=25730)
- Solid colours look shinier than multidimensional colours, and adding waves or movement disguises shine. — [news24 TrueLove](https://www.news24.com/truelove/hair/10-ways-to-make-your-wig-look-expensive-20240826)
- Dab matte eyeshadow, foundation or powder in scalp colour along the part to make the parting look realistic. — [news24 TrueLove](https://www.news24.com/truelove/hair/10-ways-to-make-your-wig-look-expensive-20240826)
- General budget cosplay-wig advice. — [Popverse](https://www.thepopverse.com/cosplay-wig-wigs-cheap-tips-tricks/)

### Inferences
- **Detangling and wig cap (general practice, not sourced in this session):**
  - Comb with a wide-tooth comb from the ends upwards. This matters especially for C.C.'s long wig, which tangles at the nape.
  - Wear a nylon wig cap over pinned-flat hair.
  - Store the wig in a hairnet or bag, not crumpled.
  - Keep heat away from non-heat-resistant costume wigs; flat-ironing a polyester Karneval wig will melt it.
- Buying a "heat-resistant fiber" wig (EZcosplay, Kasouwig) gives more styling and de-shine options than a polyester Karneval wig (e.g. Buttinette's 90% polyester).
