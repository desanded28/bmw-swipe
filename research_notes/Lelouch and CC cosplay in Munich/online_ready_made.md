## Q1. What do ready-made Lelouch and C.C. costumes cost on each platform? (listing inventory)

### Takeaway
Ready-made costumes are plentiful on eBay.de, AliExpress, Amazon.de (mostly Chinese third-party brands), and China-based "German" shops (skycosplay.de, cosplaycart.de, hellocosplay). The cheapest are AliExpress: C.C. white jumpsuit + wig from about $19 (≈€16.5), and a Lelouch Ashford uniform from about $33 (≈€28). Amazon.de complete sets sit around €68–85. Zero full costumes (suit + cape) rarely go below about €80, and a wearable Zero helmet adds about €50–120. I found no Temu, Shein or Wish listings, and no German brick-and-mortar party chains (Deiters, Karneval Universe, Kostümpalast, Animegami) appear to stock Code Geass.

## Q3. Realistic delivery to Munich by ~29 Oct 2026 (ordering Mon 12 Oct)

### Takeaway
There are 17 calendar days to 29 Oct. AliExpress "Choice" listings and eBay sellers shipping from China with SpeedPAK are a coin-flip to likely-on-time **only if ordered today and the listing's own delivery date is on or before about 27 Oct**. Made-to-order shops (skycosplay.de, CosplayFU, Hallowcos, Takerlama, cosplayworld.de) are **too risky or too late**. The safest option is Amazon.de listings that show a delivery date before 29 Oct at checkout. Many Code Geass listings there are Chinese third-party sellers whose dates may be 1–3 weeks out.

### Cited Findings

## Q4. Return policy and customs-duty/import-VAT risk for non-EU orders

### Takeaway
Since **1 July 2026**, the EU charges a flat **€3 customs duty per item category** on parcels under €150 from non-EU sellers registered in IOSS, plus 19% German import VAT. Big platforms (AliExpress, Temu, Shein, eBay with IOSS sellers) usually collect this at checkout, so it is shown in the price. Returning cheap China-shipped costumes is usually impractical. Several eBay China sellers state "no returns".

### Cited Findings
- EU Council agreed to levy a **€3 customs duty on small parcels (<€150) as of 1 July 2026**. It applies to goods from non-EU sellers registered in IOSS, which covers 93% of e-commerce flows. — [Consilium press release](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/12/customs-council-agrees-to-levy-customs-duty-on-small-parcels-as-of-1-july-2026/)
- Sources differ on how the €3 is counted:
  - Per "different item… according to tariff headings" — [Consilium](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/12/customs-council-agrees-to-levy-customs-duty-on-small-parcels-as-of-1-july-2026/)
  - Per product category, so multiple €3 charges per basket are possible — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/07/01/eu-slaps-3-duty-fee-on-shein-temu-and-aliexpress-imports)
  - Example: a t-shirt + a watch = €6 — [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/tech/eu-taxes-low-value-parcels-aliexpress-shein-temu/)
- The measure is temporary until 1 July 2028. — [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/tech/eu-taxes-low-value-parcels-aliexpress-shein-temu/)
- A proposed **€2 handling fee** may follow later in 2026. — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/07/01/eu-slaps-3-duty-fee-on-shein-temu-and-aliexpress-imports)
- Unofficial worked example for Germany: a €20 order + €3 duty, with 19% VAT on €23, totals **€27.37**. — [euparcelfee.com calculator](https://euparcelfee.com/en)
- German buyers on mydealz report AliExpress charging additional taxes since about 20 June 2026. — [mydealz thread](https://www.mydealz.de/diskussion/aliexpress-berechnet-jetzt-20062026-schon-zusatzliche-steuern-2798695)
- One buyer reported being charged the €3 customs fee plus import VAT on a German parcel. — [FindNiche](https://findniche.com/blog/aliexpress-shipping-time)
- The eBay.de Shanghai Lelouch-uniform seller says import fees are included in the price. — [eBay.de 167584371112](https://www.ebay.de/itm/167584371112)
- Several eBay costume listings say the seller **does not accept returns**, e.g. a Kallen costume listing on eBay.de. — [eBay.de 303920008099](https://www.ebay.de/itm/303920008099); [eBay 317648936777](https://www.ebay.com/itm/317648936777)
- Amazon.de shows "kostenlose Lieferung und Rückgabe" on some sizes/colours of the xhomeshop cape listing. — [Amazon.de B0B56KTHGD](https://www.amazon.de/xhomeshop-Rebellion-Halloween-Karneval-Strickjacke/dp/B0B56KTHGD)

### Inferences
- For a €17–40 AliExpress or eBay costume, expect roughly **€3 duty + 19% VAT already baked into the checkout price** when the seller is IOSS-registered. If the seller is not IOSS-registered (smaller shops or independent cosplay sites), DHL/Deutsche Post may collect VAT + duty + a carrier handling fee at the door. That carrier fee is typically several euros, but the amount is not verified here.
- Buying wig and costume as separate items from different tariff headings may trigger two €3 charges. Bundled "costume + wig" sets may be cheaper overall.
- Amazon.de is the lowest-risk channel for returns: 14-day EU withdrawal right, plus Amazon A-to-z for third-party sellers. Return shipping to China-based third-party sellers can still be awkward.
