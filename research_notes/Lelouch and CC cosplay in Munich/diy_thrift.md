> **Method note for the report writer:** Direct page fetching was blocked in this research environment. WebFetch returned DNS errors, and curl got a proxy 403 for humana-second-hand.de, oxfam.de, muenchen.de, munich.travel, in-muenchen.de, prospektangebote.de and ebay.de. **Every finding below comes from search-engine summaries and snippets of the cited pages, not from reading the pages in full.** Addresses and opening hours mostly come from directory listings (orte.muenchen.de, Cylex, kaufDA, marktguru), which can be out of date. ## 1. Which Munich second-hand / thrift stores and flea markets to use (12–31 Oct 2026), and typical prices

### Takeaway
The cheapest reliable thrift sources found are the social department stores (Diakonia Kaufhaus on Dachauer Str. 192; Weißer Rabe GebrauchtWarenHäuser in Obersendling and Westend; T-shirts there are often around €2), then Oxfam (Orleansplatz 4, Türkenstraße 81, Pasing). ReSales and Picknweight (sold by the kilo) cost more. No Humana branch in Munich could be confirmed. For flea markets in the window: Olympiapark runs every Friday and Saturday; Daglfing is confirmed for 16–17 Oct; the last M-net Hofflohmärkte are Pasing (Sat 17 Oct) and Allach (Sat 24 Oct). Riem is **not** on 17 or 24 Oct, only on Sat 31 Oct, which is Halloween day itself.

### Inferences
- Best thrift run for the black jacket, white shirt, trousers and belts: **Sat 17 Oct**, starting at Daglfing in the morning (opens 6:00), or Fri 16 Oct at Daglfing or Olympiapark. Weißer Rabe Westend (Landsberger Str. 146) and Oxfam Pasing (Spiegelstr. 11) pair well with the **Pasing Hofflohmarkt on 17 Oct**.

## 5. Cape fabric quantity, no-sew method, and tutorials (Lelouch uniform, Zero cape, C.C. straitjacket)

### Takeaway
For a no-sew half-circle cape, plan on about **1.4–1.8 m (1.5–2 yd)** for a short or mid-length adult cape and **about 4.5 m (5 yd) of 150 cm-wide fabric** for a floor-length cloak. Double that if lined. No Zero-cape-specific video was found. ### Inferences
- **No-sew Zero cape (recommended method):** Buy about 1.5–2.5 m of black fabric (felt, fleece or a thrifted black sheet or duvet cover; non-fraying fabric avoids hemming) plus the same amount of red fabric for the lining. Bond the two with iron-on hem tape or fabric glue around the edges, then add gold ribbon along the edges with fabric glue. For the high Zero collar, glue a double layer of fabric around stiff felt, craft foam or cardboard and fasten it with safety pins or Velcro. Cheapest shortcut: a €4–10 ready-made vampire cape with red lining (Kaufland and costume shops), with gold ribbon glued on.
- **Lelouch Ashford jacket without sewing:** Use a thrifted black blazer or Mao/stand-collar jacket. Tuck the lapels under and pin or glue them to fake a stand collar (a common closet-cosplay trick, not from a source above). Glue or iron gold ribbon along the collar, front edge, hem and cuffs (iron-on hem tape under a ribbon works on cotton/poly; test synthetic fabric first). Optionally swap in cheap gold buttons.
- **C.C. straitjacket without sewing:** Use a long white shirt-dress or oversized white men's shirt worn as a dress, or the two-shirt method. Wrap thrifted belts or webbing straps with D-rings around the torso, arms and thighs. Fix belt ends with safety pins or fabric glue instead of stitching (replacing the Cut Out + Keep needle-and-thread step). Long, flared sleeves can be faked by gluing a cone of white fabric inside the cuffs. Add white tights and white boots (or boot covers made from white fabric).
