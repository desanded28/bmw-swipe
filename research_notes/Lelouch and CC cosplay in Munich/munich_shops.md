# Physical shops in Munich for a Lelouch + C.C. (Code Geass) Halloween 2026 cosplay

Research date: Monday 12 Oct 2026. Halloween = Saturday 31 Oct 2026.

**Method caveat for the report writer:** Direct page fetches (WebFetch/curl) were blocked by this environment's egress proxy (policy denial / DNS failure for horror-shop.com, kostuemebreuer.de, muenchen.de). Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine result summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the pages in full. Several underlying pages are years old (flagged inline). Treat hours and stock as "call ahead to confirm".

## Which Munich stores exist (by category), where are they, when are they open, and what do they stock?

### Takeaway
Munich has one clear "walk in and buy everything" destination for Halloween/cosplay basics: **Horror-Shop.com**, with a central **City Store (Müllerstr. 54, near Sendlinger Tor)** and a big **Superstore in Landsham** east of the city. For rental of a quality cape/uniform-type outfit, **Kostüme Breuer** in Schwabing is the established Kostümverleih. For anime merch, **Neo Tokyo** (Schwabing) and **Comic Company** (Fraunhoferstr.) are the J-culture/comic shops, but no source confirms they stock cosplay wigs or Code Geass items. All shops are closed Sundays.

The best bets, in order: (1) **Horror-Shop City Store, Müllerstr. 54** (only physical shop whose catalogue includes anime/cosplay wigs, plus capes, lenses, makeup); (2) **Horror-Shop Superstore, Landsham** (same range, much larger); (3) **Kostüme Breuer, Schwabing** (71 capes, rental outfits from €49, wigs sold); (4) **feiermeier Moosach/Trudering** (costumes + wigs); (5) **Müller Karlsplatz / TEDi / Action** for cheap black capes, black wigs and makeup filler items.
