# C.C. "Empress" (Britannian outfit) build: fabrics, clothing base and accessories in Munich

Research date: Mon 12 Oct 2026. Deadline: Sat 31 Oct 2026 (Halloween). Shops are closed on Sundays. Sun 1 Nov (Allerheiligen) is a Bavarian public holiday.
Scope: fabric, clothing base, trim, gems, notions and sewing-machine access. EVA foam is covered in a separate note, and the wig was sourced elsewhere.

**Price labels**
- **[snapshot]** = search-index snapshot, verify before buying. Every sourced price comes from search-engine summaries of shop pages. No page was opened live.
- **[estimate]** = my own estimate; no source gives this figure.

## 0. Bottom line

- **Fabric is cheapest online, not in Munich shops.** I found no Munich shelf prices for fabric. German online fabric shops show white polyester satin at **€3.95/m** (Stoffkontor, buttinette), white Duchesse satin at **€6.95/m** (Stoffe Hemmers) and H250 interfacing at **€5.85–6.50/m**. They deliver in about **2–4 working days** **[snapshot]**.
  - **Order by Wed 14 Oct, Fri 16 Oct at the latest**, so the parcel arrives before the build weekend of 24–25 Oct.
- **One-basket idea:** Stoffe Hemmers stocks everything in one order: white Duchesse or felt, H250, S320, purple felt and gold foil jersey. The basket comes to about €45–50, which is near its free-shipping threshold (€50 or €60; sources disagree) **[estimate from snapshot prices]**.
- **Cheapest gold edging (my suggestion):** buy **0.5 m of gold foil-coated jersey ("Dekostoff Lamé gold")** from stoffe.de (€6.99/m, so about €3.50 for 0.5 m **[snapshot]**). Cut it into 2 cm strips across the 145 cm width. That gives roughly **36 m of non-fraying gold strip, each piece 145 cm long**, so a floor-length tail needs no join. Glue the strips on with textile glue **[estimate]**. Do not iron foil jersey directly.
- **Black gloves and white stockings:** Horror-Shop (City Store, Müllerstr. 54, or online with 1–3 working-day delivery) has black long gloves at **€6.95** and white opaque overknees at **€4.95** **[snapshot]**.
- **Sewing machine:** **HEi – Haus der Eigenarbeit** (Wörthstr. 42, Rückgebäude, 81667, Haidhausen) has a textile workshop with sewing machines and an overlocker. It costs **€18/hour**, €4.50 per 15 min, or €120 for a 10-hour card (prices valid from 1 Jun 2024) **[snapshot]**.
- **Fabric markets:** **no Stoffmarkt Holland or other fabric market in or near Munich between 12 and 31 Oct 2026** was found. The organiser's Bavaria page says none is currently announced.

## 2. White fabric for the streamers (tails) and the wing collar

### How much [estimate]
- **Tails:** 8–10 tails, each about **10–12 cm wide × 110–120 cm long** (waist to floor plus the pointed tip).
  - On 145–160 cm wide fabric, one **1.2 m length yields about 12 strips of 12 cm**. That covers all tails in a single layer.
  - Double-layer tails (front and back glued or sewn together) need **about 2.4 m**.
- **Wing collar:** two panels of about 40–45 × 50–55 cm.
  - One layer over stiff interfacing: about 0.6 m.
  - With a fabric back as well: about 1.1 m.
- **Buy:** about **2–2.5 m** for single-layer tails plus a backed collar; about **3.5 m** if every piece is double-layered.

### Material comparison
| Material | Look vs. reference | Work | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heavy satin / Duchesse** | Closest to the glossy commercial costume | Frays, so the edges need binding or glue. Needs H250 on the back for body | **Best look.** Duchesse holds its shape better than thin poly satin |
| **Felt (1–3 mm)** | Matte and craft-like; may pill | **Does not fray, so no hemming.** Glues easily | **Easiest no-sew.** 1 mm is floppy; 2–3 mm is heavy but stands well. Best for the collar |
| **Cotton canvas / twill** | Matte; crisp; takes fabric paint well | Frays; shrinks about 4% (pre-wash). Stiff on its own | Good for the wing collar and painted gold edges; dearest per metre |
| Thrifted white bedsheet | Matte | Factory hem on one edge | €2–5 **[estimate]**; see the earlier note |

### HEi – Haus der Eigenarbeit (best option)
- **Address:** Wörthstr. 42 (Rückgebäude), 81667 München-Haidhausen, near Ostbahnhof. Tel. **089 448 06 23**. — [HEi contact](https://www.hei-muenchen.de/kontakt-und-anfahrt/)
- **Textile workshop:** **7 workstations, several sewing machines, an overlocker, a leather sewing machine, ironing and cutting tables.** A **Tuesday 18:00–21:00 advisory session** runs with phone registration. — [HEi Textilwerkstatt](https://www.hei-muenchen.de/unserangebot/werkstaetten-und-kurse/textilwerkstatt)
- **Fees (valid from Sat 1 Jun 2024)** **[snapshot]**:
  - **€18 per hour** of workshop use, or **€4.50 per 15 min**
  - **10-hour card €120** (€12/h, no time limit)
  - 30-hour project card €330
  - Source: [HEi prices](https://www.hei-muenchen.de/startseite/werkstattregelung/vorstellung-preise-und-zeiten/)
- **Hours:** **Tue–Fri 15–21, Sat 12–18; closed Sun–Mon.** The city page labels these "(Hotline)", so they may be phone hours. Confirm by phone. — [stadt.muenchen.de](https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/haus-der-eigenarbeit/1082082/)
- **First visit:** one-time registration, and induction for some machines (heise 2017, dated). — [heise Make](https://www.heise.de/select/make/2017/7/1511215715233127)
- **Course:** "Nähen – ein ganzer Tag für mich" (a one-day sewing course) **€135**.
- **Status:** an older Abendzeitung article (Covid era) said HEi was "fighting to survive". The 2026 city pages still list it. — [Abendzeitung](https://www.abendzeitung-muenchen.de/muenchen/haidhausen-haus-der-eigenarbeit-kaempft-ums-ueberleben-art-550076)
- **Time and cost [estimate]:** hemming 10 tails, sewing the wing panels and attaching the waistband takes about 2–3 h, so **€36–54**.
  - Usable slots: Tue 20 to Fri 23 Oct after 15:00, Sat 24 Oct 12–18, or Tue 27 to Fri 30 Oct.
  - The **Müller at Weißenburger Str. 27** is nearby for forgotten notions.

### Other routes
- **Münchner Stadtbibliothek:** in 2020 it declined to become a "Bibliothek der Dinge" (library of things). **No sewing-machine lending was found.** — [RIS München](https://risi.muenchen.de/risi/dokument/v/6065657)
- **Nähcafés / open sewing meet-ups in Munich:** none found in search. Neighbourhood centres and the MVHS might run one (unverified).
- **Buy or borrow:** used machines appear on eBay/Kleinanzeigen München (not priced). Nähzentrum Theresienstr. 124 sells and services machines.
- **Avoid needing a machine:** felt tails + glued foil-jersey edging + hem tape make the build fully no-sew **[estimate]**.
