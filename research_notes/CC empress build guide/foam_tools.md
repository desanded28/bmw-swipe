## Takeaway

- **Foam:** Action's 6 × 40×40 cm grey EVA floor mats at **€6.97** are by far the cheapest base foam. Buy 2 packs: one for the pauldrons, belt, cuffs, collar and ornaments, and one for the wing panels.
- **Trim:** add about 5–6 sheets of 2 mm Moosgummi (about €1 per sheet) for the gold edging and the ornament frames.
- **Tools:** the only real tool cost is a **heat gun**. Borrow one from the **Münchner Werkzeugbibliothek** (Werksviertel, Atelierstr. 22, about €1–3/day), or buy a no-name one for about €16–25.
- **Cheapest must-buy total:** about **€50–60** if you borrow the heat gun, or about **€73–82** if you buy one. A household with scissors, ruler, marker, tape and cling film saves about €5–8 more. All totals are **[estimate]** built from the snapshots below.
- **Skip:** Worbla (about €65 per large sheet), Flexbond (no German source found), Mod Podge (€8–19) and a Dremel. Sand by hand, seal with PVA, and paint with Action acrylics.

## 1. Build plan and quantities [estimate]

Rough areas, from typical C.C. Empress proportions (adjust to the user's pattern):

| Part | Material | Approx. area | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pauldrons, 2 × 3 layers (about 28×22, 24×18 and 20×14 cm) | Action mat | ~2,700 cm² | Heat-form a slight curve. Bevel the edges. Edge each layer with a 2 mm strip. |
| V-belt: front V plus 2 side panels | Action mat | ~1,300 cm² | Join the panels with contact cement. Close with Velcro or elastic at the back. |
| Elbow cuffs, 2 × ~18×14 cm | Action mat, or doubled 2 mm foam | ~500 cm² | Glue or Velcro onto the black sleeves. |
| Stand collar, ~45×8 cm | 2 mm foam doubled, or a mat strip | ~360 cm² | Line with fabric. Edge with a gold 2 mm strip. |
| 8–10 eye or diamond ornaments, ~8×5 cm | 2 mm foam frame on a mat or 2 mm base | ~400 cm² | Paint the frame gold and the centre black and red. Optional gem or hot-glue "jewel". |
| Wing-collar panels, 2 × ~40×45 cm | 2nd Action pack (or 8 mm tiles), covered in white fabric or painted white | ~3,600 cm² | Mount on a harness or the collar. |
| Gold edging strips, about 11–12 m × 1–1.5 cm in total | 2 mm Moosgummi | ~1,700 cm² | About 3–4 A4-size sheets. Buy 5–6 to allow for waste. |

- **Mats:** each 40×40 mat is 1,600 cm², with about 1,400 cm² usable after the puzzle edges.
  - Armour parts (~5,200 cm²) plus ~30% waste need about **5 mats, so 1 pack**.
  - The wing panels need about **3 more mats, so a 2nd pack**.
- **Thickness:** the Action mat thickness is **not stated** in any snippet. Puzzle mats are typically ~8–10 mm [estimate], so check the pack in store.
  - 8–10 mm is fine for the pauldrons and belt. Put the smooth back face outward, or skin the textured face with 2 mm foam.
  - For thinner, lighter wing panels, the Action **"Multifunktionale Schaumstoff-Fliesen"** are an alternative: 12 pcs, **8 mm**, brown, 115×87.5 cm total, **CHF 7.49 on action.com/de-ch** [snapshot, verify]. No DE listing or price was found.

**Method summary** (standard EVA workflow):
1. Pattern with cling film plus masking tape, drawn on with a marker.
2. Transfer the pattern to the foam and cut with a fresh blade. Bevel the edges at 45°.
3. Heat-form and heat-seal with the heat gun, then join with contact cement.
4. Seal with 3–4 thin coats of PVA, then paint with acrylics. Add the gold edging last.
5. For the ornament gems, use hot glue painted red over black, or a flat-back acrylic gem.

## 5. Borrowing tools instead of buying

### Münchner Werkzeugbibliothek (Erfindergarden Foundation): cheapest heat-gun access
- **Locations:**
  - **Werksviertel, Atelierstr. 22, 81671**, two doors from boesner.
  - Lending station **Halle 2**, Peter-Anders-Str. 15, 81245 (24 h).
  - Trudering, Truderinger Str. 288.
  - The **Gasteig site closed on 30 Sep 2026**; its lending and returns moved to the Werksviertel from 1 Oct.
- **Fees:** €1–3/day, or "from €1.50/day", for private use. An annual subscription is also offered. [snapshot, verify]
- **Rules:**
  - Register online, then pay for a membership.
  - Show ID and proof of address at the first loan.
  - Maximum loan is 1 week. Reserve up to 2 weeks ahead.
  - Pick-up windows were Tue evening and Sat morning (older info).
- **Catalogue:** erfinder.myturn.com/library. **It is not verified that a heat gun or Dremel is in stock.** Check the catalogue first.
- **Sources:** [erfindergarden.de/werkzeugbib](https://erfindergarden.de/werkzeugbib/); [offene-werkstaetten.org](https://offene-werkstaetten.org/werkstatt/muenchner-werkzeugbibliothek); [rethink-muenchen](https://rethink-muenchen.de/en/offers/munich-tool-library/); [truderingimwandel](https://www.truderingimwandel.de/projekte/werkzeugbibliothek/); [city council doc](https://www.muenchen-transparent.de/dokumente/9708050/datei)

### Haus der Eigenarbeit (HEi): Wörthstr. 42, 81667 (Haidhausen)
- **Workshops:** wood, metal, ceramics, paper, jewellery, textile and upholstery, with staff support. A tool-lending service (Werkzeugverleih) is also mentioned. — [Kulturreferat doc](https://muenchen-transparent.de/dokumente/7114496/datei)
- **Workshop prices** (valid from 1 Jun 2024; may have changed) [snapshot, verify]:
  - **€18 per hour**
  - 10-hour card €120 (€12/h)
  - project card of 30 h for €330
  - supporting members (Förderverein) €12/h; membership €80–149/year
  - materials charged extra
  - Sources: [HEi prices](https://www.hei-muenchen.de/startseite/werkstattregelung/vorstellung-preise-und-zeiten/); [HEi Förderung](https://www.hei-muenchen.de/foerdern/neu-viele-optionen-zur-foerderung-des-hei/)
- **Hours:** Tue–Fri 15–21, Sat 12–18 (city page; 2018–2020 sources agree). Tel. 089 448 06 23 (2019/20 programme), welcome@hei-muenchen.de. — [stadt.muenchen.de](https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/haus-der-eigenarbeit/1082082/)
- **Use case:** one or two hours (€18–36) in the wood workshop to do all the heat-forming and sanding with their heat gun, sander or Dremel. It's worth it only if the Werkzeugbibliothek has no heat gun. Ask whether they allow contact cement or EVA in the workshop.

### FabLab München e.V.: Gollierstr. 70 (entrance D or E; sources differ), 80339 (Westend)
- **Equipment:** 3D printers, **2 laser cutters**, CNC and electronics.
- **Access:** free use after a machine introduction. Weekly **Monday** meetup; info@fablab-muenchen.de.
- **Fees and opening hours:** not found.
- **Use for this build:** laser-cut the 8–10 identical ornaments and the gold edge strips from 2 mm EVA. EVA is generally laser-safe, but **confirm with the lab; never cut PVC**.
- **Sources:** [München events](https://veranstaltungen.muenchen.de/ferienangebote-familienpass/veranstaltungen/veranstaltungsorte/fablab-muenchen-e-v); [3dnatives](https://www.3dnatives.com/de/3d-druck-branchenbuch/fablab-muenchen/)
