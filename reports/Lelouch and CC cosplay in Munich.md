# Become Lelouch and C.C. for about €60

## 1. TL;DR plan and total cost range: about €55–70 for both costumes if you order this week

Go with the season-1 pairing: **Lelouch in the black-and-gold Ashford Academy school uniform, and C.C. in her white straitjacket with a long lime-green wig and a real Pizza Hut box**. It is the cheapest combination that still reads as Code Geass, and both halves are sold cheaply online. The best-value buys found are an **AliExpress C.C. white jumpsuit that comes with a green wig for about $19–27 (≈€17–23)** and an **AliExpress Ashford uniform for about $33 (≈€28)**. The alternative uniform is an **eBay.de listing shipped from Shanghai at ≈€39, import fees already included** ([AliExpress](https://www.aliexpress.com/w/wholesale-code-geass-cc-cosplay.html); [eBay.de](https://www.ebay.de/itm/167584371112)). Add the new EU parcel duty where it isn't already in the price, a €2–4 drugstore red eyeshadow for the Geass eye and C.C.'s forehead sigil, and Lelouch's own hair. That brings the pair to **roughly €55–70**.

Money is not the constraint here; time is. A China-shipped parcel ordered on 12 Oct has only a moderate chance of arriving by Thu 29 Oct. So order **today or tomorrow**, buy only listings whose own estimate shows delivery by about 27 Oct, check tracking on Thu 22 Oct, and keep a Munich fallback ready for Sat 24 Oct. The fallback is thrift shops and flea markets for the clothes plus the Horror-Shop City Store (Müllerstr. 54) for wigs. It costs **€36–106 for both** and carries no delivery risk. Amazon.de sets that show a pre-29-Oct delivery date lower the risk but push the pair to **€130–175**. Zero is the most recognisable silhouette, but it adds cost (a DIY cape build or a ready-made suit) and the full helmet is not worth buying.

On the night, **Werksviertel-Mitte** suits an anime couple best: entry is free or cheap early on, the K-pop room gives costumed guests a free gift, and there is a Japanese karaoke bar. **WIGZ at Milla** (€18.62, costume contest) is the other strong choice. **The Animesy anime-orchestra concert on 28 Nov** (from €20, with a cosplay contest) gives the costumes a second outing.

## 2. Which outfits to pick: the Ashford uniform and the straitjacket give the most recognition per euro

Lelouch has three realistic looks, and they differ mainly in price. His **Ashford Academy uniform** is a black, gakuran-style jacket with gold trim, gold lining and the Ashford logo on the collar, worn over a white long-sleeve shirt with black trousers and black shoes ([Code Geass Wiki](https://codegeass.fandom.com/wiki/Ashford_Academy)). The jacket has gold piping and gold buttons ([SoraNews24](https://soranews24.com/2015/12/16/slip-into-some-loungewear-that-makes-you-look-as-chic-as-lelouch-from-code-geass/)).

His alter ego **Zero** wears:
- a tight **dark-purple suit** with gold stripes and a frilled white cravat (a lighter purple in R2);
- a black-and-gold cloak with a **crimson lining**;
- a spiky black-and-indigo helmet with a small panel that slides down to expose his **left eye** ([Code Geass Wiki – Zero](https://codegeass.fandom.com/wiki/Zero)).

Shop listings that describe Zero as a "blue suit with yellow accents" with a white mask don't match the show ([Yinocosplay](https://www.yinocosplay.com/products/code-geass-zero-cosplay-costume)). The white-and-gold **Emperor** outfit from the R2 finale is sold as a set of robe, cape, hat, jewelled belt and gems ([eBay](https://www.ebay.com/itm/255087680308)). It costs **€148 and up** ready-made ([hellocosplay](http://de.hellocosplay.com/code-geass-c-4_11.html)), and custom builds take pre-orders at **US$1,360** ([GNG Cosplay](https://gngcosplay.shop/products/emperor-lelouch-from-code-geass-cosplay-outfit-re-order)), so it is out of this budget.

C.C. is defined by her **long lime-green hair and gold eyes**. Her season-1 signature is the **white straitjacket "bondage suit"** that Britannia used to restrain her ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.C._(Code_Geass))). It has many straps and zippers, a high collar, and long sleeves that flare out towards the cuffs ([Code Geass Wiki](https://codegeass.fandom.com/wiki/C.C.)). Sources disagree on strap colour: one seller's jacket has purple straps, while an Etsy pattern uses two black straps with silver buckles ([Cosrea](https://www.cosrea.com/products/code-geass-cc-straitjacket-cosplay-costume); [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/992964429/cc-straight-jacket-sewing-pattern)). Whatever belts you find cheaply will do.

C.C. has three other outfits worth knowing about:
- Her **Ashford girls' uniform** is a light yellow jacket with gold trim and black cuffs, a white shirt, a dark green tie and a short black skirt ([Code Geass Wiki](https://codegeass.fandom.com/wiki/Ashford_Academy)). It is mostly sold as a "Shirley" costume.
- Her **pilot suit** is white with gold accents, gold gloves and thigh-high boots ([eBay](https://www.ebay.com/itm/186886895251)). It is the least practical option.
- The props carry the joke. Pizza Hut sponsored the show in Japan, C.C. keeps ordering pizza on Lelouch's credit card, and she hugs a **Cheese-kun** plush, which is Pizza Hut Japan's mascot ([ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/code-geass-adult-swim-anime-pizza-hut-closure/); [Manga Wiki](https://manga.fandom.com/wiki/C.C._(Code_Geass))). Her Geass sigil glows on her **forehead** ([Manga Wiki](https://manga.fandom.com/wiki/C.C._(Code_Geass))), which a red eyeliner reproduces for almost nothing.

**Ashford Lelouch with straitjacket C.C.** is the canonical season-1 "first meeting" pair. Both pieces turn up cheaply, new and second-hand, and C.C. carries the recognition for both of you. **Zero with straitjacket C.C.** is the most photogenic pairing and the one most likely to win a costume contest. Its mask, though, is the most expensive and least comfortable piece. If you go Zero, wear the cape all night and use the mask as a photo prop. **Both in Ashford uniforms** is the cheapest and comfiest option, but it needs the pizza box and the Geass makeup to read as Code Geass at all.

Whatever you choose, the **lime-green wig is the most valuable single item**. It turns any white outfit into C.C., and a used one was listed for €10 ([Kleinanzeigen](https://www.kleinanzeigen.de/s-cc-code-geass/k0)).

Neither costume is warm. Munich's daily lows fall from about 7 °C to about **3 °C** over October ([WeatherSpark](https://de.weatherspark.com/m/70344/10/Durchschnittswetter-im-Oktober-in-M%C3%BCnchen-Bayern-Deutschland)). Every party below is indoors, so plan around coats and the cloakroom rather than a warm costume. C.C.'s short Ashford skirt is the coldest choice and needs opaque tights.

## 3. Option A: ready-made online — AliExpress is cheapest, Amazon.de is the only low-risk channel

Searches found no Code Geass products at the German party chains (Deiters, Karneval Universe, Kostümpalast, Animegami), and no Temu, Shein or Wish listings surfaced. "Ready-made" therefore means Chinese factory costumes sold through four kinds of seller:
- AliExpress;
- eBay.de;
- Amazon.de third-party brands such as Gooyeh, Zhongkaihua, Xinnice and Xianyuee;
- China-based shops on .de domains.

The Amazon.de brands are very likely the same factory products as on AliExpress at a 2–4× markup. What the markup buys is faster delivery and an easier return. Dollar prices are converted at about **0.86 €/$**, the rate implied by an eBay.de listing that shows US$19.00 as €16.35 ([eBay.de](https://www.ebay.de/itm/285597127607)).

### Delivery: China-shipped orders must go in by Tuesday 13 October

There are 17 days between ordering on 12 Oct and an arrival target of Thu 29 Oct. Estimates for AliExpress Choice to Germany vary:
- **5–14 days**, according to one 2026 guide ([FindNiche](https://findniche.com/blog/aliexpress-shipping-time));
- **10–15 working days**, according to a German logistics blog ([Logistik-Journal](https://www.logistik-journal.de/aliexpress-lieferzeit/));
- for all international parcels to Germany in August 2026, half arrived within 9 days and most within 3–18 ([ParcelsApp](https://parcelsapp.com/en/shipping-reports/destinations/germany)).

AliExpress Choice gives a coupon if the delivery is late, which compensates you but leaves you without a costume.

**skycosplay.de** ships from China despite its domain. A standard order takes 7–12 days of production (15–20 around Halloween) plus 8–15 days of delivery. Even the paid "24H" option, from €14.99, still adds 8–15 days in transit, which means 21–29 Oct at best ([skycosplay Versandzeit](https://skycosplay.de/pages/versandzeit)). One eBay buyer of a Lelouch uniform reported a Halloween order arriving weeks late, with no reply from the seller ([eBay](https://www.ebay.com/itm/317648936777)).

### Customs, sizing and returns

Since **1 July 2026** the EU has charged a **€3 customs duty** on parcels under €150 from non-EU sellers ([Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/12/customs-council-agrees-to-levy-customs-duty-on-small-parcels-as-of-1-july-2026/)). It is counted per product category, so one basket can attract several charges ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/07/01/eu-slaps-3-duty-fee-on-shein-temu-and-aliexpress-imports)). 19 % import VAT applies on top, which turns a €20 order into about **€27.37** ([euparcelfee.com](https://euparcelfee.com/en)). German buyers report AliExpress adding these charges at checkout since about 20 June 2026 ([mydealz](https://www.mydealz.de/diskussion/aliexpress-berechnet-jetzt-20062026-schon-zusatzliche-steuern-2798695)). This guide budgets **≈€3.60 per item** (the duty plus VAT on it) on top of AliExpress prices. Prefer a single listing that bundles costume and wig over buying them separately.

Sizes are Asian and run small. Sellers advise ordering **one size up** and allowing 1–3 cm of tolerance ([Amazon.de Zhongkaihua](https://www.amazon.de/Zhongkaihua-Rebellion-Erwachsene-Rollenspiel-Halloween/dp/B0D7W4K35B)). Check Lelouch's sleeve length and the torso length of C.C.'s jumpsuit.

Returning a cheap costume to China is impractical, and several eBay costume sellers refuse returns outright ([eBay.de](https://www.ebay.de/itm/303920008099)). Amazon.de is the one channel where a return is realistic, thanks to the EU 14-day withdrawal right and Amazon's A-to-z guarantee.

### Four no-sew builds

**Lelouch's Ashford jacket**
- Start with a thrifted black blazer or stand-collar jacket. A used Primark black blazer sold for €5 on eBay ([eBay.de](https://www.ebay.de/itm/376599010330)).
- Fold the lapels under and pin or glue them to fake a stand collar.
- Iron or glue gold ribbon along the collar, front edge, hem and cuffs. Swap in gold buttons if you like.
- If you'd rather sew, a pattern-based Ashford jacket guide covers the shape ([yousai.net](https://yousai.net/english/howto/cos/code_l.htm)).

**Zero's cape**
- Fabric: a long adult cape takes about **2 yards (1.8 m)** of non-fraying fabric ([The Homes I Have Made](https://thehomesihavemade.com/20-minute-super-cape-tutorial/)). A floor-length semicircle takes about **5 yards (4.6 m) of 150 cm-wide fabric** ([Bucknell](https://eg.bucknell.edu/~lwittie/sca/garb/cloak.html)). Double either amount for a lining.
- Build: bond a black outer layer to a red lining with hem tape or fabric glue, then glue gold ribbon round the edges.
- Collar: stiffen it with felt, craft foam or card, because standing collars flop over a long night ([Instructables](https://instructables.com/Spawn-Cape)).
- Shape: cosplayers describe Zero's anime cape as "incredibly angular" and recommend heavy interfacing. Their budget alternative is a long black trench coat with a purple shirt and gold trim ([The Cosplay Tutorial Blog](https://blog.cosplaytutorial.com/post/39710694633/i-want-to-cosplay-zero-from-code-geass-but-i-have)).
- Shortcuts: a thrifted black duvet cover already has two layers. The laziest option is a red-lined vampire cape with gold ribbon glued on; new vampire capes start at €3.99 plus shipping ([Kaufland.at](https://www.kaufland.at/product/520648876/)).

**C.C.'s straitjacket**
- Two published tutorials build a straitjacket from **two button-down shirts and 6–8 belts**, attached to the back, front and sleeves ([Instructables](https://www.instructables.com/How-to-Make-a-Strait-Jacket/)). One takes under 30 minutes, with thin black belts stitched into the sleeve ends ([Cut Out + Keep](https://www.cutoutandkeep.net/projects/simple-straight-jacket)). Safety pins or fabric glue can replace the stitching.
- Use an oversized white men's shirt or a long white shirt-dress, and glue a cone of white fabric inside each cuff to get the flared sleeves.
- Finish with white tights and white boots or boot covers.
- A digital C.C. straitjacket sewing pattern costs about $5 on Etsy ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/code_geass_costume)).

**C.C.'s Ashford uniform**
- Thrift a cream or pale-yellow blazer, a white blouse, a short black skirt and a dark green tie.
- Gold ribbon on the blazer edges does the rest.

To make a cheap wig look less like one:
- Wash it before wearing, because new synthetic wigs are at their shiniest.

### Geass eye: one €3–13 red lens, or a €2 red eyeshadow

Plain red lenses without prescription are cheap from German shops:
- **Funnylens** sells cosplay and anime lenses from **€2.99 to about €12.99 a pair**, such as the "Anime Arc 3 Red" ([Funnylens](https://www.funnylens.de/anime-cosplay-kontaktlinsen)).
- The **OTTO** marketplace lists red lenses without prescription from about **€7 a pair** ([OTTO](https://www.otto.de/gesundheit/kontaktlinsen/?farbe=rot&s=ohne-sehstaerke)).
- **LuxDelux** sells a red yearly lens for €13.99–19.99 ([LuxDelux](https://luxdelux.de/Itachi-rote-Motivlinsen-Cosplay-Farbige-Kontaktlinsen-Crazy-N-13-ohne-Staerke)).

The only true Geass-sigil lens found, Mojosee's at US$34.98, is a non-EU product whose "FDA-cleared" claim is unverified ([Mojosee](https://mojosee.com/products/lelouch-lamperouge-cosplay-contact-lenses)). Skip it. Lenses come in pairs, but Lelouch needs only one, in the **left eye**, the one the Zero mask's panel exposes.

In the EU, coloured lenses without corrective power fall under MDR Annex XVI and must meet safety requirements similar to medical devices ([Johner Institut](https://www.johner-institut.de/blog/regulatory-affairs/produkte-ohne-medizinische-zweckbestimmung/)). German eye-care bodies add their own advice:
- The opticians' association ZVA advises a professional fitting even for a few hours' wear ([Handwerksblatt](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/panorama/vorsicht-bei-farbigen-kontaktlinsen-zu-halloween)).
- The German Ophthalmological Society (DOG) warns of corneal injury and infection. It advises wearing them for only a few hours and taking them out at once if the eyes redden, burn or water ([Ärzte Zeitung](https://www.aerztezeitung.de/Medizin/Problematischer-Hingucker-308740.html)).
- Pharmacists stress washing your hands first and never sharing lenses ([aponet](https://www.aponet.de/artikel/grusel-linsen-zu-halloween-darauf-sollten-sie-bei-kontaktlinsen-achten-12094)).

For one night, choose a lens labelled for daily wear and ask a Munich optician for a quick fitting check. The zero-risk alternative is red eyeshadow around one eye. For C.C.'s gold eyes, only a US-sold yellow lens turned up ([colored-contacts.us](https://colored-contacts.us/c-c-cosplay-yellow-contact-lenses/)), so leave them out.

### Practical tips for the night

- **Carry a repair kit.** Pack safety pins, a few spare belt loops and a small tube of fabric glue. Glued ribbon and pinned straps are the first things to fail on a crowded dance floor.
- **Do makeup last and keep it away from the white suit.** Put on C.C.'s red sigil after she is dressed, and have Lelouch set his eye makeup with powder before he leans on her shoulder.
- **Take the lens out early.** Remove Lelouch's lens before the 01:00–03:00 stretch, and never let anyone try it on.
- **Pick up the pizza box on the day.** It's a prop, not dinner: buy it on the way, eat the pizza, and keep the box flat and clean.
- **Bring coats and ID.** Use the cloakroom for the coats, and bring ID, because WIGZ is reportedly 16+ and LOKOO is 18+.

### The calendar, not the wallet, decides this plan

The surprising finding is how little the costumes themselves cost. The recommended pair (€54–70) costs about the same as two tickets to a mid-priced Halloween party. What actually constrains the plan is the 17-day delivery window. That turns the thrift run on 17 Oct into insurance: it costs maybe €20–30, has no downside if the parcels arrive, and saves Halloween if they don't.

Recognition is also lopsided. C.C. carries the couple through her green wig and pizza box, so money spent on her wig buys more recognition than anything spent on Lelouch, whose cost can fall close to zero. If you want to step up later, the order is: wig first, then a DIY Zero cape, and the helmet never. A second outing at The Animesy on 28 Nov roughly halves the effective cost of either upgrade.
